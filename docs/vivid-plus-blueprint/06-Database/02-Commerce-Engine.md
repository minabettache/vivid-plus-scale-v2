# Commerce & Loyalty Engine

## Purpose

The Commerce Engine is the financial source of truth for VIVID+.

It records every verified customer interaction that creates measurable business value.

This data powers:

- Customer Lifetime Value
- Repeat Revenue
- Loyalty
- Rewards
- AI Recommendations
- Campaign Attribution
- Revenue Forecasting
- Business Health Score

---

## Core Principles

### Immutable Financial History

Transactions are never edited.

Corrections are made using adjustment records.

---

### Ledger-Based Loyalty

Point balances are cached.

The Loyalty Ledger is always the source of truth.

---

### Verified Revenue

Only completed transactions contribute to:

- Revenue
- Average Ticket
- Customer Value

---

### Business Isolation

Every record belongs to exactly one business.

---

### Multi-location Ready

Transactions belong to locations.

Reports can aggregate across businesses.
